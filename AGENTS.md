# Continuity Film Studio operating rules

1. Generate no production video until the visual bible is approved and every asset used by the shot is locked.
2. Give each character, state variant, location, and key prop one versioned passport. Never shorten its descriptor in a prompt.
3. Never rename a reference file. Create a new version instead.
4. Keep raw generations in `generations/`; only checklist-approved takes enter `selects/`.
5. Change at most one prompt block between attempts. At attempt 15, simplify or split the shot.
6. Keep required on-screen text out of model generation and add it during edit.
7. Do not commit production media, identity references, credentials, or provider tokens.
8. Confirm source, likeness, voice, music, and distribution rights before commercial output.
9. Treat model names as live provider data. Discover the catalog; do not rely on a stale article or README.
10. Editing, cleanup, color, sound, and mastering retain a human approval gate.
