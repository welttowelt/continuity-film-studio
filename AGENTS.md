# Continuity Film Studio operating rules

1. Treat the pinned Machina submodule as the source for the seven creative skills. Do not rewrite their doctrine.
2. Generate no production video until the visual bible is approved and every asset used by the shot is locked.
3. Give each character, state variant, location, and key prop one versioned passport. Never shorten its descriptor in a prompt.
4. Never rename a reference file. Create a new version instead.
5. Keep raw generations in `generations/`, where only the prompt engineer works. The edit stage reads only checklist-approved takes in `selects/`.
6. Change exactly one prompt line between attempts and keep every other line verbatim. Rejected attempts 10 to 15 call for a simpler shot, and 15 is the hard stop.
7. Keep required on-screen text out of model generation and add it during edit.
8. Do not commit production media, identity references, credentials, or provider tokens.
9. Confirm source, likeness, voice, music, and distribution rights before commercial output.
10. Treat model names as live provider data. Discover the catalog instead of relying on a stale article or README.
11. Editing, cleanup, color, sound, and mastering retain a human approval gate.
